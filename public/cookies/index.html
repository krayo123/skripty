Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

KrayoSkriptz uses cookies, browser storage, scripts, and similar technologies to load the website, keep admin sessions working, show external media, and display ads. This page explains the main categories.

Essential Used by hosting and the browser to deliver pages, assets, security checks, and basic site functions.
Admin session Supabase Auth can store login/session data in the browser for the private admin page.
Advertising Third-party ad providers can use cookies, local storage, scripts, redirects, and measurement tags.
External media Services such as YouTube or Lootlabs may set their own cookies when previews, videos, or links are opened.

Advertising Cookies

Advertising scripts may be loaded from third-party networks. These providers can use identifiers to measure ad delivery, limit repeated ads, detect invalid traffic, and personalize campaigns. Their own privacy and cookie policies apply when their scripts run.

Managing Cookies

You can delete cookies, block third-party cookies, or restrict scripts in your browser settings. Blocking advertising scripts can make some advertising frames fail to load or trigger anti-adblock messages.

Changes

This page can be updated when new ad networks, analytics tools, login features, or external embeds are added to the site.
